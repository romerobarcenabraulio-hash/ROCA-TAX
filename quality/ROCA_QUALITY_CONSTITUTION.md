# ROCA Quality Constitution

Estado: ACTIVE FOR RECONSTRUCTION

## Fitness for purpose

ROCA Audit succeeds when a person can understand how ROCA operates, what each area must look like, how work moves, what evidence proves a condition, what remains open, and where the supporting source lives — without reading a giant duplicated book.

## Non-negotiable quality scenarios

Q-01 Source preservation
A stronger supported fact cannot disappear merely because a later source is shorter.

Q-02 Canonical home
A requirement, fact or decision has one canonical home. Repetition is a reference, not a second independent truth.

Q-03 Area integrity
Each operational area can be understood as a complete working system: governance/handoff, physical area, method, assets, materials, people/qualification, records/evidence and audit.

Q-04 Operator communication
Workshop personnel see direct observable actions and conditions. Legal/normative prose remains in the management assurance layer.

Q-05 Evidence semantics
A photo, document, measurement, interview or BIWO event proves only the claim it actually supports.

Q-06 Physical-digital consistency
Piece identity, physical stage and BIWO state do not tell conflicting stories.

Q-07 Privacy
Sensitive client, employee and legal originals do not leak into public Git or publication output.

Q-08 Editorial quality
The final artifact does not look like a dashboard, AI scaffold or consulting template. Photography, diagrams, tables and prose each earn their use.

Q-09 Print/browser parity
HTML and PDF are two renderings of the same controlled content; neither becomes a parallel source of truth.

Q-10 Release honesty
No complete/final/verified/liberable claim is allowed without direct evidence from the relevant gates.

## Anti-theater rules

A large page count is not completeness.
A green build is not release approval.
A photo count is not evidence coverage.
A checklist is not compliance.
A commit is not verification.
A newer file is not automatically the strongest source.
More agents are not better if they duplicate ownership.

## Required release gates

1. source integrity;
2. content-loss comparison;
3. canonical-home/duplication check;
4. area-integrity coverage;
5. technical/process coherence;
6. standards/applicability provenance;
7. evidence semantics;
8. privacy;
9. editorial lint;
10. browser interaction QA;
11. visual regression QA;
12. print/PDF QA;
13. critical open-action check.

Release verdict remains binary:
PASS - LIBERABLE
BLOCKED - NO LIBERAR
